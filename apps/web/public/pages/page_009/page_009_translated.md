**[EN]:**
The Crawl Mode is for those who want to play a game that focuses on killing monsters and gaining loot, without worrying about a continuing story. 
When playing the Crawl Mode, players start by choosing a Scenario. There are 5 different Scenarios in Unintentional Malum: Act 1, each with a Rank 
associated with it. The Rank of the Scenario determines the amount of Gold and XP the Adventurers start the Scenario with. Higher Rank Scenarios will 
give the Adventurers an increased amount of Gold and XP to compensate for the difficulty of the enemies they will be facing. If this is your first time 
playing, we recommend starting with the Rank 1 Scenario, Cavern Fumigation.

**[PT-BR]:**
The Crawl Mode is for those who want to play a game that focuses on killing monsters and gaining **Saque/Saques**, without worrying about a continuing story. 
When playing the Crawl Mode, players start by choosing a Scenario. There are 5 different Scenarios in Unintentional Malum: Act 1, each with a Rank 
associated with it. The Rank of the Scenario determines the amount of Gold and XP the **Aventureiros** start the Scenario with. Higher Rank Scenarios will 
give the **Aventureiros** an increased amount of Gold and XP to compensate for the difficulty of the enemies they will be facing. If this is your first time 
playing, we recommend starting with the Rank 1 Scenario, Cavern Fumigation.

**[EN]:**
If players would like to make the Crawl Mode provide a more in-depth 
experience, you can try playing the Epic Crawl Mode. In this mode, players 
will start a Rank 1 Scenario as a novice, and eventually end at the highest 
Rank Scenario you own in your Middara collection.  
To play the Epic Crawl Mode, start a Rank 1 Scenario of your choice. After 
completing it, keep all your Gold, Items, and XP. Then, perform a Buy Round 
where you purchase new items at the next Loot Level (Common for Rank 
2) and spend XP to purchase new Disciplines. After spending XP and Gold, 
choose a Scenario from the next Rank up. Meaning, players may never play a 
Scenario of the same or lower Rank twice.
Players will never Gain XP or Gold listed in the corresponding Crawl 
Scenarios after the first Rank. If you’d like to pause your Epic Crawl run, you 
can do so between Ranks. Use the normal rules from the Adventure Mode by 
storing your characters, Items, and Disciplines in a Tuck Bag. You can also use 
an Adventure Sheet to keep track of the current party's Gold and XP. 
If players are ever Defeated, they lose the Epic Crawl. If they win a Rank 4 
Scenario (or a Rank 7 if you have expansions) you’ve won this Epic Crawl run!

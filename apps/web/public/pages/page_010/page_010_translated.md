<!-- BLOCK 4: [86.17, 233.23] 318.97x99.94pt -->
<!-- FONTS: OptimaLTStd-Italic 10pt ITALIC, OptimaLTStd 10pt -->
**[EN]:**
Every new Adventure starts with a Scene. These Scenes give context to 
the characters and the world the players are experiencing, as well as 
detailing the story of the Adventurers. Scenes are sections of narrative that 
are read by the players between Encounters. These sections should be 
read aloud to the table so that all players will be able to experience what 
is happening in the Adventure. Alternatively, players may choose to read 
scenes individually. For those interested in listening as a group, you can 
find our Adventures in an audio format on the Forteller Games App, or as 
a set of MP3 files from www.Middara.com.

**[PT-BR]:**
Every new Adventure starts with a Scene. These Scenes give context to 
the characters and the world the players are experiencing, as well as 
detailing the story of the **Aventureiros**. Scenes are sections of narrative that 
are read by the players between Encounters. These sections should be 
read aloud to the table so that all players will be able to experience what

<!-- BLOCK 6: [86.17, 365.18] 314.65x88.9pt -->
<!-- FONTS: OptimaLTStd 10pt -->
**[EN]:**
Occasionally, while reading through a Scene, players will be asked to 
perform various tasks. These are called Adventure Mechanics. These can 
be Skill Checks, Gaining new party members, adding Story Flags to the 
Adventure sheet, or instructing players to continue to the next Scene or 
Encounter. In short, Adventure Mechanics are special rules that players 
must perform when instructed to. Adventure Mechanics are always listed 
in a box so that players can easily identify them. Players must finish 
performing a listed Adventure Mechanic before proceeding.

<!-- BLOCK 8: [86.17, 487.08] 320.64x88.9pt -->
<!-- FONTS: OptimaLTStd-BoldItalic 10pt BOLD ITALIC, OptimaLTStd 10pt, OptimaLTStd-Italic 10pt ITALIC -->
**[EN]:**
The players win an Encounter when they reach a Win Condition. Players 
lose an Encounter if they reach a Lose Condition first. All Win and Lose 
Conditions will be listed in the Encounter Setup of each Encounter. If the 
players ever win or lose an Encounter, they read the Hidden Text under 
the corresponding Condition of the Encounter Setup. This Hidden Text 
will instruct the players how to proceed. If players reach a Lose Condition 
first, they still keep all the rewards they have Gained during the Encounter 
such as Gold and Items. See End Conditions and Rewards Pg. 14.

**[PT-BR]:**
The players win an Encounter when they reach a **CONDIÇÃO DE VITÓRIA**. Players 
lose an Encounter if they reach a **CONDIÇÃO DE DERROTA** first. All Win and Lose 
Conditions will be listed in the Encounter **Preparação** of each Encounter. If the 
players ever win or lose an Encounter, they read the Hidden Text under 
the corresponding Condition of the Encounter **Preparação**. This Hidden Text 
will instruct the players how to proceed. If players reach a **CONDIÇÃO DE DERROTA** 
first, they still keep all the rewards they have Gained during the Encounter 
such as Gold and Items. See **Condições Finais** and Rewards Pg. 14.

In addition to the Adventure Sheet, separate Adventurer Tuck Bags are also

are not equipped by any Adventurer in the party.

<!-- BLOCK 16: [415.84, 465.95] 316.57x66.9pt -->
<!-- FONTS: OptimaLTStd 10pt, OptimaLTStd-Bold 10pt BOLD -->
**[EN]:**
In Adventure Mode, players have access to an entire party, not just an 
individual Adventurer. At the beginning of most Adventures, players will 
have just enough Adventurers in their party to play with the minimum 
amount of supported players (usually 4). However, as the Adventure 
progresses, new Adventurers may join the party, while others may end up 
leaving.

**[PT-BR]:**
In Adventure Mode, players have access to an entire party, not just an 
individual Adventurer. At the beginning of most Adventures, players will 
have just enough **Aventureiros** in their party to play with the minimum 
amount of supported players (usually 4). However, as the Adventure 
progresses, new **Aventureiros** may join the party, while others may end up 
leaving.

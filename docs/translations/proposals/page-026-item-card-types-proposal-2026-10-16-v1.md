## Exceção de estrutura

### p26-block-478-symbol-abilities-body

**EN:** Some Abilities printed on Items require Symbols to use. Unless otherwise specified, Symbols that add TO THE ATK ROLL may only be spent during the Spend Symbols and use Abilities to add to the Attack Roll step of an Attack. Likewise, Symbols that add to Damage can only be spent during the Spend Symbols and use Abilities that add Damage step of an Attack. See section 6 of The Attack Action Pg. 41.

**PT-BR proposto:** Algumas Habilidades em Itens exigem Símbolos. Salvo indicação em contrário, Símbolos que adicionam à ROLAGEM DE ATAQUE só podem ser gastos na etapa de um Ataque para Gastar Símbolos e usar Habilidades que somem à Rolagem de Ataque. Símbolos que adicionam Dano só podem ser gastos na etapa de um Ataque para Gastar Símbolos e usar Habilidades que somem Dano. Consulte a seção 6, A Ação de Ataque, p. 41.

**Motivo:** The long body is split into three non-contiguous typographic span units around the bold “TO THE ATK ROLL” and “may only” runs. The visible parent key page_26_block_52 does not select in the Admin Editor. A partial override would separate the sentence around those runs and risks incoherent flow or overlap; the body remains in source English until one body unit and selection boundary are available.

**Ação:** Provide one selectable editorial unit for the full body while retaining its inline emphasis, then verify the complete translation in Viewer, read-only Editor and one-page PDF.
